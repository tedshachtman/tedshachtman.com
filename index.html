Questions

What parents usually ask.

When should my student start SAT prep?

A good window is two to four months before the test date, often in 10th or 11th grade after a PSAT score comes back. The practice test at the start shows how much time makes sense.

How will I know it’s working?

You’ll get a short note after every session. SAT students retake a full practice test every few weeks, and school-subject students bring in their graded work, so we can track progress with real numbers.

Do you teach younger kids too?

Yes. I trained as an elementary teacher and have taught kindergarten through 5th grade in public schools and Hebrew school. Sessions for younger students can be shorter and more hands-on.

Can sessions be online?

Yes. Online sessions use video with a shared whiteboard, and they work well for SAT prep and coding. You can also mix in-person and online weeks.
